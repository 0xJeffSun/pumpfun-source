## Controls and ordering actually reviewed

Administrative Global contexts bind signer through has_one/constraints and canonical PDA. QuoteControl has a distinct permission lattice: Global alone assigns list admin, Global/list admin assign reserves admin and mutate membership, and all three may update reserves. Permissionless collection/distribution sends to destinations derived from stored keys/config, rather than caller-selected arbitrary keys. Holder distribution is expressly allocation-authority controlled and does not independently prove holders' entitlement. Those are candidate control descriptions, not complete proofs against all caller graphs.

Typed Program/Interface accounts pin System, Token families, ATA, AMM, Fees and Metaplex where used; manual CPI instructions were inspected for their actual program_id expressions, account order, signer flags and PDA seeds. Unchecked `program` event accounts were not automatically labeled arbitrary targets: for example migrate_v2 constructs `program_id:crate::ID`. Generated event_cpi expansion and all transitive callees remain outside this source-only review. Bounded call-target matches in prior documents are not substituted for that work.

Late validation is common: buy-v2 validates buyback after base output and tops up rent after events; migrations create a pool before checking boost remaining accounts and later token-mint writability; holder distribution validates final rent after payouts; route destination mint is checked after every hop. `?` propagates these errors. The security argument depends on atomic runtime rollback, including CPIs, and on consumers not interpreting logs of failed transactions as committed events. This pass runs no transaction to establish that premise and does not call ordering alone a vulnerability.

Arithmetic reviewed includes checked add/sub/narrow helpers, ceiling-fee rounding, floor buyback/share allocation, denominator guards, saturation, casts and panics. For u64 factors, `(2^64-1)^2+9999 < 2^128`, so the ceiling-fee intermediate alone does not overflow u128. This bounded fact is not a proof that rates, reserves, per-recipient casts or every route composition are valid. Release profile specifies overflow checks and abort panic; explicit errors and panic paths remain distinguishable.
