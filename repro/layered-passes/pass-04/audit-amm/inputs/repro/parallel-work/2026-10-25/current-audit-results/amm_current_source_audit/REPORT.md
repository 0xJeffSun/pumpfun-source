### F03 invariant/composition reconciliation

Let R be actual quote balance, F accrued protocol+creator fees, V signed virtual quote, q curve quote sold, l LP fee, p protocol fee, c creator fee, b buyback. In a successful ordinary-token sell, user payout=q-l-p-c and buyback=b, while accrued increment=p-b+c. Thus R'=R-(q-l-p-c)-b and F'=F+p-b+c, so **R'-F'=(R-F)-(q-l)**. Splitting buyback does not repair that equation. Accrual changes V by the negative accrued increment, so V+F is locally preserved. Sweep decreases R,F and increases V by the same paid amount, preserving R+V and V+F, but cannot prove the precondition R>=F for arbitrary preceding sells. The legacy sell has a separate backing bound; v2/hop lacks the same explicit gate.

[Boost:26–88](inputs/programs/pump-amm/src/boost.rs#L26) can transfer quote into the boost vault and set positive V after authorized conditions. Therefore pricing reserve R+V and spendable backing R-F are different. Nonnegative resulting physical token balance alone does not imply nonnegative R'-F'. This does not furnish a reachable violating trade: initialization, pool balances, LP scaling, fee settings, authorization and all preceding transitions must be composed first.

The [nested pricing invariant study](inputs/repro/parallel-work/2026-10-25/invariant-results/pump_nested_pricing/REPORT.md) treats Pump nested pricing/wide arithmetic, not all boosted AMM v2 sell transitions. The [wide contracts](inputs/repro/parallel-work/2026-10-25/composition-results/pump_route_wide/CONTRACTS.md) and [settlement study](inputs/repro/parallel-work/2026-10-25/composition-results/pump_route_settlement/REPORT.md) retain positive-denominator, ordinary-token, transfer/error-order/account-admission premises. None proves q-l<=R-F across reachable AMM states. This is a precise applicability limit, not rejection of those packages' local arguments.
