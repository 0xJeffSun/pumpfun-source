## Why3002 remains separate

W217's mismatch branch creates the name/message and Source origin before wrapping and then replaces its origin with the owned10byte `IdlAccount` account name. It includes formatter failure, allocator failure and origin-replacement cleanup. Although the outer parser later replaces that name, those earlier allocations and drop operations remain part of the path. Current bare3002 plus static account-name adapter does not recover them. This proposal neither fabricates the captured filename/line nor silently changes that branch to a superficially equivalent final log. Its full original body is preserved for later source attribution. A later3002 proposal needs legitimate source-origin construction and complete replacement-drop proof; this3001 branch requires neither.
