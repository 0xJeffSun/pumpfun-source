This does not establish authentic complete source, proposal preservation or binary equality. No build, runtime, new fixture, main change, network, transaction or git mutation was performed.

All immediate settlement callees are covered; selected deeper CPI and borrow helpers are included. Calls leaving these intervals remain an explicit unresolved frontier. Error-formatting/metadata bodies are byte inventory only, with no semantic recovery of other handlers. No excluded source implementation or probe was opened or resumed.

The installed v1.44 LLVM objdump was used only as a disassembler. Its instruction indexes were multiplied by eight to display virtual addresses; every displayed raw instruction is independently checked against ELF section mappings. Its human-readable `<.text+...>` labels are retained as historical tool output but are **not authoritative**: they can be offset from the encoded destination. The ledger computes `pc + 8 + 8*signed_offset` for branches and the corresponding signed immediate for direct calls. ELF relocations override apparent relative call operands.
