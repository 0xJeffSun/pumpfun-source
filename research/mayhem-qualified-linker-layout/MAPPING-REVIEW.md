# Qualified-layout mapping audit

`translation.py` independently verifies the baseline-to-reordered candidate movement for both stripped and symbol ELFs. It derives the 456-section bijection from the two linker maps, predicts all function bytes after CALL/relocated-pointer adjustment, checks 4,929 direct calls, all 2,542 relocations, 445 data pointers (74 moved), all allocated data bytes, symbol values and the entrypoint. `.rodata` is unchanged; `.data.rel.ro` changes only through the mapped pointers. REL records change order but have exactly the expected translated multiset. No ELF is written. This establishes preservation of the rebuilt baseline, not identity with the original deployment.

`mapping_review.py` records why the proposed original placement remains heuristic. The 13 logged conflicts reduce to four distinct tuples. Two contradictions already follow from unique-shape callers, so changing the greedy tie-break cannot eliminate them:

* O0232 (`std::io::Error::new`, 176 bytes) is a single original target, while a unique Curve decoder requires candidate 494952 and six other unique caller bodies require candidate 565680. Both candidate bodies match locally but have distinct StringError vtables. Their allocation/call structure alone does not authorize merging those address identities.
* O0246 (`RawVec::grow_one`, 408 bytes) is required at candidate 592576 by 127 callsites in unique generated-account helpers, and at 566280 by two callsites in unique SPL transfer. The clones use distinct, locally equal 312-byte `finish_grow` bodies. Those leaf bodies have the same actual immediate targets and no relocated data, but global call-sharing still differs.

The maximum one-to-one shape assignment is **427**, although **428** original windows have at least one matching candidate shape. O0209 and O0237 both need a 328-byte `finish_grow`, but there is only one such candidate section. This is also a contextual gap: none of the four candidate 640-byte reserve-helper bodies calls the 328-byte child required by original O0210. Their children are 656/312/312/312 bytes. The existing plan's O0209→312-byte role assignment is therefore explicitly unequal; swapping in the only 328-byte child would displace O0237 and would still not repair O0210's actual call. Ordering cannot generate the missing specialization.

The qualified plan has 14 unequal body bindings: the 13 unresolved original bodies plus this one multiplicity deficit. That is consistent with the shape-cardinality upper bound; it is not a proof of 441 recreated bodies.

For future mapping trials, retain explicit candidate domains and separate exact shape, actual-call, typed-data and source-role evidence. Propagate caller constraints only when their domains agree; emit a contradiction witness when a unique caller forces incompatible targets. Use injective matching for the unresolved equal-shape domains and keep unequal source-role hypotheses visibly separate. Candidate order is a deterministic heuristic, not original-placement evidence.

To remove the hard contradictions, a source/toolchain change must recover the original sharing/specialization graph, or a carefully validated compiler/linker folding configuration must do so. Blanket folding is insufficient because some candidate clones need merging while the 328-byte specialization needs another distinct instance. No instruction masking, padding or output patch is justified by this audit.
